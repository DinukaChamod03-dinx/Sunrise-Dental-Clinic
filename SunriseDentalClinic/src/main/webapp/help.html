Staff Instructions

Step-by-step guide to using the clinic system.

01

Logging in

Use the username and password given to you by the clinic administrator. If you enter incorrect details, the system will display an error message and allow you to try again.

02

Registering a new appointment

From the dashboard, choose "Register Appointment". Enter the patient's name, address and contact number. Select the dentist, treatment type, appointment date and time. The system will automatically create a unique appointment number and prevent double-bookings for the same dentist and time slot.

03

Finding an appointment

Choose "Search Appointment" from the dashboard. Enter the exact appointment number or type the patient's name. Partial patient names can also be used. Then click the Search button to display matching records.

04

Managing appointments

Open "All Appointments" to review every clinic booking. From this section, staff can update patient details, edit appointments, mark visits as completed after treatment, or cancel bookings when necessary.

05

Billing a patient

Open "Billing", enter the appointment number and click "Generate Bill". The system automatically calculates the treatment charge together with the standard consultation fee. Use "Print Receipt" to provide the patient with a printed copy.

06

Logging out

Always click "Logout" when you have finished using the system. You can also select "Exit System" from the dashboard. This is especially important when using shared computers to protect patient information.

!

Need more help?

Contact the clinic administrator if you are unable to log in, require a password reset, encounter an unexpected system error, or need assistance with a clinic operation.

🔐

Patient Data Security

Never share your login details with another person. Always log out after completing your work, especially when using a shared clinic computer.
