Help & Instructions

A quick guide for new front-desk staff

1

Logging in

Use the username and password given to you by the clinic administrator. If you enter the wrong details, the system will show an error and let you try again.

2

Registering a new appointment

From the main menu choose "Register Appointment". Fill in the patient's name, address, contact number, choose the dentist and treatment, and pick a date and time. The system automatically creates a unique appointment number and blocks double-bookings for the same dentist and time slot.

3

Finding an appointment

Choose "Search Appointment" and type the exact appointment number, or a patient's name (partial names work too), then press Search.

4

Managing appointments

Open "All Appointments" to see every booking. From there you can edit patient details, mark a visit as completed once treatment is done, or cancel an appointment if the patient does not show up.

5

Billing a patient

Open "Billing", enter the appointment number, and click "Generate Bill". The system automatically calculates the treatment cost plus the standard consultation fee. Click "Print Receipt" to print a copy for the patient.

6

Logging out

Always click "Logout" (or "Exit System" from the main menu) when you are finished, especially on shared computers, so patient data stays secure.

!

Need more help?

Contact the clinic administrator if you are locked out, see an unexpected error, or need a password reset.
